name: Plain-language website copy
description: The user wants clear, simple wording across the furniture storefront.
---

Use common words and short sentences across customer-facing pages, especially About, Services, Projects, Why Us, and Questions. Prefer direct labels over marketing jargon. Keep product facts and legal terms intact when simplifying.

**Why:** the user asked.

**How to apply:** When editing storefront copy, replace difficult marketing language with clear, direct wording without changing confirmed facts or the meaning of legal terms.
