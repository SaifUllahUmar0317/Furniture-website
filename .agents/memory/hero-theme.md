---
name: Homepage hero theme and image treatment
description: Preserve the hero photo while adapting overlay and foreground contrast to light and dark themes.
---

The homepage hero should look light in light theme and dark in dark theme without washing out or obscuring its furniture background image.

**Why:** The user wants the image to remain recognizable and visually important in both themes, with the hero's colors changing along with the rest of the site.

**How to apply:** Use theme-aware translucent overlays and semantic foreground colors; keep blur and overlay opacity restrained enough that the furniture scene remains recognizable.
